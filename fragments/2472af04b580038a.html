A SpaceX IPO at $1.75 trillion likely forces the market to look beyond rockets and start repricing the full supply chain beneath the space economy.

Here's the map, sector by sector:

Tweet 2 — Spacecraft & Launch Systems

Spacecraft & Launch Systems

• $RKLB ($84.42) — Only Western small-launch vehicle with proven orbital heritage. Neutron targeting first flight H2 2026. $816M Space Force NSSL contract and $190M HASTE hypersonics deal on the books.

• $FLY ($43.60) — Alpha back in flight. Blue Ghost lunar lander on the surface. $372M FORGE program in U.S. missile-warning infrastructure. Nvidia partnership flying AI compute on a late-2026 lunar mission. $1.4B backlog.

• $LUNR ($27.54) — Only commercial company to land and operate on the lunar surface. Five NASA CLPS task orders including the $180M IM-5 mission.

• $RDW ($10.39) — In-space manufacturing and the optical nav system flying on Artemis II. ESA QKDSat spacecraft prime and $44M DARPA VLEO contract.

• $YSS ($35.26) — Standardized satellite buses at half the cost of legacy primes. 1,000+ spacecraft/year capacity. $545-595M revenue guidance for 2026.

Tweet 3 — Satellite Operators & Services

Satellite Operators & Services

• $ASTS ($86.76) — Space-based cellular broadband direct to unmodified smartphones. BlueBird 7 launches April 19. Targeting 45-60 satellites on orbit by year-end.

• $PL ($38.55) — Largest Earth observation constellation in operation. Selected as a prime under the $151B SHIELD IDIQ. Record backlog approaching $900M.

• $IRDM ($41.63) — Only truly global LEO satellite network with pole-to-pole coverage. NTN Direct entering beta in 2026 for D2D connectivity.

• $BKSY ($37.68) — Gen-3 constellation delivering 35cm imagery with AI-enabled change detection for NGA. $99M government IDIQ secured.

Tweet 4 — Raw Materials & Mining

Raw Materials & Mining

• $MP ($60.88) — Only scaled rare earth mining and processing operation in the U.S. Pentagon backstopped a $1.25B magnet manufacturing campus with a 10-year price floor.

• $AA ($65.72) — Primary aluminum supplier feeding launch vehicle airframes and spacecraft structures across the Western supply chain.

• $FCX ($70.06) — Copper underpins every power system and electronic subsystem in the space stack. Largest publicly traded copper producer globally.

Tweet 5 — Specialty Materials & Alloys

Specialty Materials & Alloys

• $ATI ($165.46) — Titanium and nickel superalloys for propulsion and airframes. Guiding to $1B adjusted EBITDA for 2026.

• $CRS ($445.00) — High-performance nickel superalloys in structural shortage. Record Q2 operating income of $155M. Guiding $660-700M adjusted operating income for FY26.

• $HXL ($88.55) — Carbon fiber composites in launch vehicles, satellite platforms, and pressure vessels. Supplies 100+ active space and defense programs including Artemis.

Tweet 6 — Electronics & Semiconductors

Electronics & Semiconductors

• $QRVO ($82.11) — SATCOM beamformer ICs for phased-array terminals and satellite payloads. Pending Skyworks merger creates a $22B RF platform.

• $ADI ($372.48) — Precision analog and beamforming silicon with a dedicated Commercial Space Products program qualified for LEO and GEO missions.

• $COHR ($347.47) — Laser-based optical interconnects for satellite-to-satellite and ground comms. Pentagon building laser comms into next-gen space architecture. Nvidia just took a $2B stake.
