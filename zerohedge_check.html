Method notes

  • Free articles only. Only free ZeroHedge articles were read for this page. A figure from a subscriber-only article is not used here, in any form.
  • As-of rule. A stock figure (a backlog, a balance) is compared with the latest filed value dated on or before the article. A flow (revenue, capex) is compared with the filed period the article names or, where it names none, the latest reported period at the article's date. Figures are compared as stated: adjusted is not set against reported.
  • Same-day relay. A figure ZeroHedge published within two days of the company's own filing or release is flagged. An exact match there mostly shows the release being relayed, so the page gives the shares with and without those rows.
  • Verdicts. Confirmed means a filing states the same figure within 2%; close means within about 10%. Misses are mostly scope (adjusted against reported, a commitment against cash funded) rather than invented numbers.
  • Limits. The held filings carry fiscal years and the 2026 quarters, so 2025 quarters cannot be checked. Industry figures are modelled results with no filed counterpart. Periods are inferred from the article's wording and date, so a fiscal-against-calendar slip is possible.
