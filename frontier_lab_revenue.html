Frontier labs are consuming the majority of AI CAPEX but are private. Funding round valuations and reported ARR are the only public signals. Implied EV/Revenue multiples of 15–30× represent the 'AI premium' the market is pricing in — the key question is whether revenue acceleration justifies the multiple expansion.
