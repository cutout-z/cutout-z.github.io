What it shows. Aggregate tokens processed per day through the OpenRouter API (prompt + completion, native tokenizers), summed across the top 50 models and the long-tail other bucket. Why it matters. Token throughput is the closest public proxy for real LLM adoption and workload growth — free of benchmark self-reporting. Caveats. OpenRouter traffic only, not whole-market; volumes include free-tier models, so token share ≠ spend share; the top-50 membership can change day to day.
