Renewal & obsolescence

Hardware & facility

Whether the shell can house the hardware of 2035–2045. Accelerators turn over every year or two while a building is designed for decades, and successive generations have drawn more power per rack and moved cooling from air towards liquid; packaging, memory, interconnect and the supply chain behind them set the pace. An option in 2040 is exercised only if the shell can house the hardware of 2040, so the trajectory is measured against a stated tier-1 design envelope (AirTrunk/CDC class), never against an actual facility.

How it is measured
Formula
Fit ln(rack kW) = a + b × year by least squares over the table's rows (one per accelerator generation: the densest rack configuration the vendor publishes; year = the availability year the vendor stated at announcement, placed at mid-year). Crossing year t* = (ln E − a) ÷ b, E = the tier-1 envelope in kW per rack. Value = t* − as_of, in years: positive is the headroom left at the fitted rate; zero or negative means the fitted trend passed the envelope that many years earlier (shown negative, never floored at zero). Change = value now − value at the previous table edit, each from the table as it stood at that edit. as_of = the date of the newest edit.
Inputs
content:app/content/hardware_envelope.py: ROWS: generation, vendor, year, rack_kw_typical, cooling, source_url, ENVELOPES: kw_per_rack, cooling, operator, source_url, EDITS: date
Refresh
On vendor roadmap events; Zalen edits the table · owner Zalen · stale after 180 days
Notes
The table's 4 NVIDIA rack configurations (2018–2027), fitted log-linear, grow 37% a year; the fitted trend reaches the envelope in 2028.9, and the newest row, Rubin Ultra NVL576 (Kyber rack) (2027), is stated at 600 kW per rack. The envelope, 600 kW per rack with liquid cooling, is the stated design capability of CDC Data Centres (CDC update, Infratil Investor Day, ASX filing of 18 Sep 2025 (p. 131), linked in the table: “CDC's cooling technologies enable cutting edge +600 kW high-density and high floor loading for advanced compute workloads.”), never any facility's fit-out; AirTrunk publishes no kW figure and NEXTDC's own pages disagree. One table edit so far, so no earlier value; perf per watt is on GPU Hardware & Pricing, and AMD has no row because it states no rack-level power.
