Australian commitment

The anchor tenants' stated intent for Australia: hyperscaler investment announcements, site leads by evidence tier, and the megawatts in the project database linked to a hyperscaler. A tenant still committing capital and sites in Australia expects to need the capacity it leases there; a slowdown in announcements and leads would show before a slowdown in leasing, and so before a renewal is in doubt. Announcements are statements of intent, not contracted leases, and are read as such.

How it is measured
Formula
Announced capital = Σ capex_aud_m ÷ 1,000 over every announcement dated on or before the newest announcement_date, in A$bn (an announcement with no figure is counted but adds nothing). Change = the last step in the sum: the capital the newest announcement with a figure added, measured from the announcement with a figure before it (the previous watermark, and the date the row's change runs from); a register that has only one figure falls back to the capital newly announced in the 3 months to the date, else 1 month. With it: the cumulative sum at each quarter end, by company; site leads counted by evidence_tier (the lead register has no date, so a level, never a change); and linked MW = facility_mw in projects.parquet of each project an announcement's linked_project names (matched on project_name, and on operator = linked_operator where a name repeats). as_of = the newest announcement_date.
Inputs
data/au_dc/reference/hyperscaler_announcements.csv: announcement_name, parent_company, announcement_date, capex_aud_m, linked_project, linked_operator
data/au_dc/reference/hyperscaler_site_leads.csv: site_name, parent_company, evidence_tier, state, facility_mw, critical_it_mw
data/au_dc/processed/projects.parquet: project_name, operator, status, facility_mw
Refresh
AU lane: weekly rebuild of the manual seeds · owner AU lane (Hermes curates the seeds) · stale after 60 days
Notes
6 announcements dated 24 Oct 2023 to 28 Sep 2026, 3 with an A$ figure (A$50.0bn in all; 4 announced since 14 Jun 2025, to 28 Sep 2026, adding A$25.0bn); the figures are multi-year statements of intent, some capital and operating spend together, not contracted leases. 10 site leads (tier A 5, tier B 3, tier C 2) carry no date, so they show a level, not a change. The project database has no hyperscaler flag, so the MW counted in it are those of the projects an announcement links, 550 MW in all: NEXTDC S7, 550 MW facility (Proposed), linked 05 Dec 2025.
