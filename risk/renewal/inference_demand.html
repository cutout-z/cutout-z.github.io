Renewal & obsolescence

Inference demand

How much AI work is actually being run: token volume, and consumer and enterprise adoption of AI services. AI workloads in Australian data centres are inference and cloud rather than training, so this is the demand a renewal depends on: a tenant renews capacity that paying workloads still fill. The token evidence here is OpenRouter traffic, which is developer and aggregator routing rather than enterprise cloud volume, so it reads as developer mindshare, not market share; frontier-lab revenue is the paid-adoption side.

How it is measured
Formula
Open share = Σ total_tokens of models the map classes as open weights ÷ Σ total_tokens of models it classes as open or closed, over the 4 complete weeks (Monday to Sunday, the last ending on or before the file's newest date) to the newest complete week; 4 weeks because a single week swings 3–7 pts as models launch and fade. Unclassified tokens (OpenRouter's long-tail `other` row, codename models under stealth/ and openrouter/, and named models the map does not cover) are left out of both sides, so a codename model's launch week does not move the share, and are reported as a share of all tokens. The map, app/content/openrouter_model_map.py, gives each OpenRouter model open or closed weights and the ZeroEval model_id whose licence it was read from (proprietary → closed, unknown → not classified, any other licence → open, as R1), or an override with its reason; the leaderboard itself is not read here. Change: against the same 4-week share ending 13 weeks (3 months) earlier, else 4 weeks earlier. as_of = the newest date in the file.
Inputs
data/external/openrouter_rankings_daily.csv: date, model, total_tokens
content:app/content/openrouter_model_map.py: slug, weights, zeroeval_id, reason
Refresh
Daily OpenRouter rankings pull · owner LaunchAgent com.zalen.openrouter-usage · stale after 14 days
Notes
OpenRouter is developer and aggregator traffic, not enterprise cloud volume (Azure, Bedrock, Vertex): this reads as developer mindshare, not market share. The share covers the 4 complete weeks 07 Sep to 04 Oct 2026; 18.8% of their tokens are unclassified and left out (OpenRouter's unnamed long-tail row 6.3%, codename models 9.4%, named models the map does not cover 3.1%); in the window the change is measured against, 21.0% were. The map classifies 319 OpenRouter models, 242 from ZeroEval's licence field and 77 by a stated override.
