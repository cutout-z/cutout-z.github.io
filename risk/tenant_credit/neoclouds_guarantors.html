Tenant credit

Neoclouds & guarantors

CoreWeave, Lambda, Nebius and the other GPU clouds that lease DC capacity and rent compute on to labs and hyperscalers, and the guarantors, NVIDIA among them, who stand behind some of those leases. A neocloud pays from GPU rental income on hardware that ages faster than the lease runs, and from its own customers' payments. A guarantee moves the credit to the guarantor, whose fortunes depend on the same AI demand; the more leases share one guarantor, the more the market's credit concentrates in that name.

How it is measured
Formula
Largest guarantor's share = Σ amount_usd_bn in force of the guarantor backing the most ÷ Σ amount_usd_bn of all guarantees in force, on the register's newest date (as_of, from the date column). In force: dated on or before the date and not past date + term_years; a row with no term_years counts as in force. Amounts as announced, not amortised. The series is the largest share at each quarter end and on as_of, whoever leads on each date; the change is against the largest share 3 months before (else 1 month, else at the previous announcement). With it: Σ in force by guarantor on every date that changes (each announcement, each term end), and Σ announced by guarantor in each quarter.
Inputs
data/reference/guaranteed_commitments.csv: date, guarantor, obligor, beneficiary, amount_usd_bn, term_years, source
Refresh
Monthly curation pass (Hermes), as the funding-deal register · owner Hermes · stale after 90 days
Notes
6 guarantees in force on 17 Aug 2026, $187.3bn from 3 guarantors; NVIDIA backs $111.3bn of it. Three months earlier, on 17 May 2026, NVIDIA was also the largest, at 66.3%. A guarantee counts as in force from its announcement date until its term runs out, and without end when no term is disclosed. 1 with no term disclosed ($43.8bn, 23% of the total) is counted as in force. Amounts are as announced, not amortised over the term.
