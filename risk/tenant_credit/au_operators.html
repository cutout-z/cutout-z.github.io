Tenant credit

Australian operators

NEXTDC, Goodman, Macquarie Technology, AirTrunk, CDC, Equinix and the other owners and operators of Australian data centres, seen as a market: the companies that borrow against long-dated tenant leases. An operator's leverage, its funding cadence and the tenants its capacity is contracted to set how much lease income can fall before debt service is strained. The node follows them as market participants, through listed financials and announced raises, debt facilities and contracts.

How it is measured
Formula
Median, across the listed operators, of debt to equity: total debt ÷ shareholders' equity, in %, from each one's latest reported balance sheet, as the AU lane's Yahoo Finance quotes carry it (NXT, GMG and MAQ at present; others as they list). Per operator with it: EV/EBITDA and market capitalisation. As of: the newest date in financials_history.parquet, written in the same run as the quotes, which carry no date. Net debt ÷ EBITDA and capex ÷ operating cash flow are not in these files, so they are not computed.
Inputs
data/au_dc/processed/financials_quotes.parquet: ticker, name, debt_to_equity, ev_ebitda, market_cap
data/au_dc/processed/financials_history.parquet: date
Refresh
AU lane: weekly quotes; ratios move with semi-annual statements · owner AU lane · stale after 200 days
Notes
The median of 3 ASX-listed operators' debt to equity (GMG, MAQ, NXT), each from its latest reported balance sheet, dated 28 Sep 2026 by the newest week of the price history. The quotes file is replaced on every weekly run and keeps no history, so there is no earlier value to measure a change from; net debt ÷ EBITDA and capex ÷ operating cash flow are not in the AU lane's files. Goodman's ratio is the whole group's, most of it industrial property.
