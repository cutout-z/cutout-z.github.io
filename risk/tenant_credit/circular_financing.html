Tenant credit

Circular financing

Loops in which one company is funder, customer and guarantor at once: a chip vendor or cloud provider invests in a lab or neocloud, the money returns as spending on the investor's compute, and the investor or a related party backs the lease or the debt. The loop ties balance sheets together, so stress at one propagates through the lease to the others, and a guarantee from a party already exposed to the same tenant is worth less, because its capacity to pay falls when the tenant's does.

How it is measured
Formula
Circular share = Σ amount_bn of deals marked is_circular ÷ Σ amount_bn of all tracked deals, over the 12 months to the newest deal. With it: Σ circular amount by entity and by quarter. Stale after 90 days from the later of the newest deal and the last monthly curation pass (fetcher_log.json, monthly-curation-pass.last_run), which checks the register whole.
Inputs
data/reference/funding_deals.csv: date, entity, amount_bn, type, is_circular, counterparty
data/fetcher_log.json: monthly-curation-pass.last_run
Refresh
Monthly curation pass (Hermes); the news catalogue carries the announcements · owner Hermes · stale after 90 days
Notes
30 deals dated in the 12 months to 08 Sep 2026, 18 marked circular: $404.1bn of $557.4bn. A trailing window: a large deal leaving it moves the share as much as a new one entering. Checked in the curation pass of 3 Oct 2026; newest deal 8 Sep 2026.
