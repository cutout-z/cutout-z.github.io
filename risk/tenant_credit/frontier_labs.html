Tenant credit

Frontier labs

OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral and the other model developers that sign multi-year compute and capacity commitments, directly or through a neocloud. A lab whose committed compute spend outruns its revenue is a weaker lessee, and a weaker counterparty to any guarantor behind it; funding rounds finance any gap, so their cadence and size are read alongside revenue. Open-weights progress cuts both ways: bearish for frontier-lab margins, which weakens the lab as a tenant (this node), and bullish for token volume and data-centre demand, which Renewal & obsolescence tracks.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ latest compute_commitments ÷ Σ latest arr, both USD bn from frontier_lab_valuations.csv, over the labs with both (of OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and Mistral), each lab's latest row on or before the date. compute_commitments is the lab's announced cumulative total; as_of is the newest date among the rows the sum uses. Per lab: commitments, ARR, the date each was stated, and commitments ÷ ARR. The series and the change are over the same labs: the series from the first date they all had both, the change 3 months back where they all had both then, else 1 month, else since the previous observation.
Inputs
data/reference/frontier_lab_valuations.csv: date, company, metric, value, unit, notes
Refresh
On announcement; Hermes's monthly curation pass · owner Hermes · stale after 120 days
Notes
OpenAI and Anthropic, the labs with both series: $1,013.2bn of announced compute commitments (OpenAI at 22 Jul 2026, Anthropic at 1 Oct 2026) against $135.0bn of ARR (OpenAI at 29 Sep 2026, Anthropic at 31 Jul 2026). Three months earlier, on 1 Jul 2026, the multiple was 12.1× (commitments: OpenAI at 27 Feb 2026, Anthropic at 20 Apr 2026; ARR: OpenAI at 1 Feb 2026, Anthropic at 31 May 2026). xAI ($20.0bn committed) and Mistral ($2.3bn committed) have no ARR series, so they are in the chart and table but not the sum. Commitments are announced totals, most over multi-year terms, and ARR is one year's run rate, so the multiple reads as years of current revenue committed.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ USD bn raised in the 12 months to the register's newest deal (the as-of date: the newest date in funding_deals.csv) by the labs with an ARR series ÷ Σ their latest ARR on or before that date (frontier_lab_valuations.csv, metric arr). A raise is a funding_deals.csv row whose entity is OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI or Mistral AI itself and whose type is equity, debt, hybrid, debt_convertible, ipo or revolver; balance_sheet spending, acquisitions and vehicles a lab anchors (Stargate JV) are not its raises. Per lab: months from the last raise to the as-of date, the gap before it, and raise ÷ ARR at the time (the latest ARR on or before the raise date; — where there is none).
Inputs
data/reference/funding_deals.csv: date, entity, amount_bn, type
data/reference/frontier_lab_valuations.csv: date, company, metric, value
Refresh
Monthly curation pass (Hermes); the news catalogue carries the announcements · owner Hermes · stale after 90 days
Notes
OpenAI and Anthropic, the labs with an ARR series, raised $217.0bn in the 12 months to 08 Sep 2026 against $105.0bn of ARR. xAI ($50.0bn) and Mistral ($3.8bn) have no ARR series, so they are in the chart and table but not the multiple. The register begins 21 Jan 2025, so the multiple is drawn only for 12-month windows it covers in full; a round leaving the window moves it as much as a new one entering.
