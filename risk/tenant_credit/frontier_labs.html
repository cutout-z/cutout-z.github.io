Tenant credit

Frontier labs

OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral and the other model developers that sign multi-year compute and capacity commitments, directly or through a neocloud. A lab whose committed compute spend outruns its revenue is a weaker lessee, and a weaker counterparty to any guarantor behind it; funding rounds finance any gap, so their cadence and size are read alongside revenue. Open-weights progress cuts both ways: bearish for frontier-lab margins, which weakens the lab as a tenant (this node), and bullish for token volume and data-centre demand, which Renewal & obsolescence tracks.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ latest compute_commitments ÷ Σ latest arr, both USD bn from frontier_lab_valuations.csv, over the labs with both (of OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI and Mistral), each lab's latest row on or before the date. compute_commitments is the lab's announced cumulative total; as_of is the newest date among the rows the sum uses. Per lab: commitments, ARR, the date each was stated, and commitments ÷ ARR. The series and the change are over the same labs: the series from the first date they all had both, the change 3 months back where they all had both then, else 1 month, else since the previous observation.
Inputs
data/reference/frontier_lab_valuations.csv: date, company, metric, value, unit, notes
Refresh
On announcement; Hermes's monthly curation pass · owner Hermes · stale after 120 days
Notes
OpenAI and Anthropic, the labs with both series: $1,013.2bn of announced compute commitments (OpenAI at 22 Jul 2026, Anthropic at 1 Oct 2026) against $135.0bn of ARR (OpenAI at 29 Sep 2026, Anthropic at 31 Jul 2026). Three months earlier, on 1 Jul 2026, the multiple was 12.1× (commitments: OpenAI at 27 Feb 2026, Anthropic at 20 Apr 2026; ARR: OpenAI at 1 Feb 2026, Anthropic at 31 May 2026). xAI ($20.0bn committed) and Mistral ($2.3bn committed) have no ARR series, so they are in the chart and table but not the sum. ARR is each lab's annualised run rate as it states it or as reported (the latest period's revenue scaled to a year): a point in time, not a forecast, not contracted, and not adjusted for churn. Commitments are announced cumulative totals, most over multi-year terms. The multiple therefore reads as years of current revenue the commitments represent; it is a constructed proxy, not a standard credit ratio.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ USD bn raised in the 12 months to the register's newest deal (the as-of date: the newest date in funding_deals.csv) by the labs with an ARR series ÷ Σ their latest ARR on or before that date (frontier_lab_valuations.csv, metric arr). A raise is a funding_deals.csv row whose entity is OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI or Mistral AI itself and whose type is equity, debt, hybrid, debt_convertible, ipo or revolver; balance_sheet spending, acquisitions and vehicles a lab anchors (Stargate JV) are not its raises. Per lab: months from the last raise to the as-of date, the gap before it, and raise ÷ ARR at the time (the latest ARR on or before the raise date; — where there is none). Stale after 90 days from the later of the newest deal and the last monthly curation pass (fetcher_log.json, monthly-curation-pass.last_run), which checks both registers whole.

Notes
