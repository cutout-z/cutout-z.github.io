Tenant credit

Hyperscalers

Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Oracle and Meta: the anchor tenants against which long-dated DC leases have historically been written, and the baseline the rest of this row is read against. The node keeps that baseline in view so a change in it is noticed: capex taking a larger share of operating cash flow, free cash flow narrowing, or net cash moving towards net debt as the AI build is funded. A change in an anchor tenant's capacity to pay reaches every lease it holds, including those in Australia.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ capex ÷ Σ operating cash flow across Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Oracle and Meta, each at its latest reported fiscal year; the change is against each one's previous fiscal year. Per company: capex ÷ operating cash flow, FCF = operating cash flow − capex, net debt = total debt − cash and equivalents (negative = net cash), and capex summed over its last four reported quarters. Annual, not the latest quarter: the inputs carry no quarterly operating cash flow. Each fiscal year is dated by its year-end (the period of its annual capex row in quarterly_financials); the as-of is the newest of the five year-ends.

How it is measured
Formula
Σ amount_usd_bn of each company's newest row in leases_not_commenced.csv on the date, for Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Oracle and Meta: undiscounted future lease payments as each 10-Q or 10-K states them, each at its own balance date; as_of is the newest of the five. The series is the same sum at each calendar quarter end, where all five have a figure dated on or before it; the change is against the sum 3 months before as_of (else 1 month). Beside it, not added: operating + finance lease liabilities on each balance sheet (present value, SEC XBRL, lease_liabilities.csv), and venture and guarantee claims curated from the same filings, listed with what each overlaps. CoreWeave and Nebius are listed, not counted.
