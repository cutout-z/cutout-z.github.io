Supply, demand & power

Australian pipeline

Australian data-centre capacity from announced to operating, by region and status: risked MW, absorption, pre-commitment and workload mix. Capacity built against signed contracts carries renewal risk; capacity built on spec carries lease-up risk first. If absorbing what can actually be built needs demand growth faster than any published scenario, renewals are negotiated from weakness. Only pipeline with secured power can become competing supply. The workload mix shows how much of the market depends on AI inference rather than traditional and cloud demand.

How it is measured
Formula
Needed growth = (D ÷ A)^(1 ÷ (H − Y)) − 1, in % a year. A = the latest actual NEM data-centre consumption (TWh in Y, the last Baseline year of dc_demand_forecasts.csv); H = the last year all three scenarios reach; D = the TWh that would run the operating stock plus the pipeline dated by H at a mature utilisation: D = Σ MW × k × u × 8,760 ÷ 10^6, over NEM regions (NSW1, QLD1, VIC1, SA1, TAS1). MW = risked_mw (the project database's existing risk weights; risk_base_mw for the unrisked case); k = PUE for MW recorded as IT load or a campus envelope, 1 for MW recorded as facility power (capacity_basis power_consumption_mw, gross_power_mw, grid_connection_mva). u = 50%: AEMO's mature-fleet utilisation of connection capacity (AEMO, 2026 ESOO (Aug 2026), p. 36: 45–51% by data-centre type, around 50% in aggregate; 45% and 51% are shown too: https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/electricity/nem/planning_and_forecasting/nem_esoo/2026/2026-electricity-statement-of-opportunities.pdf?rev=4f2e73fbd906456288198fdbc24b335a&sc_lang=en). PUE = the median pue of the NEM projects with MW that state one (value and count in the notes). A pipeline project (any status but Operating) is placed at its full_capacity_year, else startup_year, else H, and no earlier than Y + 1; one dated after H is left out. Each scenario: growth = (its TWh in H ÷ A)^(1 ÷ (H − Y)) − 1, and utilisation = its TWh ÷ the TWh of the stock plus pipeline at full use. The scenarios are national, so regions are a breakdown, not compared one by one; the regional ESOO supply surplus is not used, as its maximum demand already includes AEMO's own data-centre forecast. as_of = the newest last_verified_at in projects.parquet.
Inputs
data/au_dc/processed/projects.parquet: nem_region, status, risked_mw, risk_base_mw, capacity_basis, pue, startup_year, full_capacity_year, last_verified_at
data/au_dc/reference/dc_demand_forecasts.csv: year, scenario, dc_consumption_twh
Refresh
AU lane: weekly rebuild of the project DB · owner AU lane · stale after 60 days
Notes
NEM regions: 38 operating projects record 1,641 MW (25 more disclose no MW and add nothing); the pipeline to 2032 is 2,001 MW risked of 6,908 MW unrisked; the pipeline outside the NEM (WA, 200 MW risked) shows only by region. full_capacity_year is recorded for 1 of 27 NEM pipeline projects, so 15 are placed by startup_year and 11 undated ones (378 MW risked) at 2032; as AEMO notes new facilities take five to ten years to reach a mature rate, the comparison is made at 2032, not year by year. PUE 1.32 is the median of the 49 NEM projects with MW that state one; at AEMO's 45–51% the needed growth is 24.3–26.6% a year, and on the 2025 actual the operating stock runs at 21% (AEMO: 27% of connection capacity in quarter 1 2026). No earlier value: the project database keeps no vintages and the scenarios are fixed.

How it is measured
Formula
Per operator: contracted_capacity_mw ÷ (operating_capacity_mw + under_construction_mw + future_build_mw), a blank part counted as 0, or ÷ total_capacity_mw where the row gives no breakdown; each operator's latest dated contracted figure is paired with its latest dated capacity figure. Disclosing share = their capacity ÷ the capacity the file states across all its operators (each operator's latest figure). as_of = the newest announcement_date among the rows the market figure uses. How it is measured
Formula
AI share = Σ MW of pipeline projects whose workload_type is AI/HPC or Cloud/AI ÷ Σ MW of all pipeline projects, in %; pipeline = every status but Operating (Proposed, Approved, Under Construction). Beside it, the same share of operating stock (status Operating). Cloud/AI mixes AI and cloud workloads and is counted as AI, shown on its own in the table. A project with no workload_type is its own class (not recorded), never Traditional, and is not AI. MW per project = facility_mw (the database's stated capacity), else critical_it_mw, else it_load_mw, as S3; a project with none is counted as without a figure and adds no MW. By region: MW per workload class per nem_region, operating and pipeline. as_of = the newest last_verified_at. No change yet: the file keeps no earlier vintages.
