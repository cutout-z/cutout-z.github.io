Supply, demand & power

Australian pipeline

Australian data-centre capacity from announced to operating, by region and status: risked MW, absorption, pre-commitment and workload mix. Capacity built against signed contracts carries renewal risk; capacity built on spec carries lease-up risk first. If absorbing what can actually be built needs demand growth faster than any published scenario, renewals are negotiated from weakness. Only pipeline with secured power can become competing supply. The workload mix shows how much of the market depends on AI inference rather than traditional and cloud demand.

How it is measured
Formula
Needed growth = (D ÷ A)^(1 ÷ (H − Y)) − 1, in % a year. A = the latest actual NEM data-centre consumption (TWh in Y, the last Baseline year of dc_demand_forecasts.csv); H = the last year all three scenarios reach; D = the TWh that would run the operating stock plus the pipeline dated by H at a mature utilisation: D = Σ MW × k × u × 8,760 ÷ 10^6, over NEM regions (NSW1, QLD1, VIC1, SA1, TAS1). MW = risked_mw (the project database's existing risk weights; risk_base_mw for the unrisked case); k = PUE for MW recorded as IT load or a campus envelope, 1 for MW recorded as facility power (capacity_basis power_consumption_mw, gross_power_mw, grid_connection_mva). u = 50%: AEMO's mature-fleet utilisation of connection capacity (AEMO, 2026 ESOO (Aug 2026), p. 36: 45–51% by data-centre type, around 50% in aggregate; 45% and 51% are shown too: https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/electricity/nem/planning_and_forecasting/nem_esoo/2026/2026-electricity-statement-of-opportunities.pdf?rev=4f2e73fbd906456288198fdbc24b335a&sc_lang=en). PUE = the median pue of the NEM projects with MW that state one (value and count in the notes). A pipeline project (any status but Operating) is placed at its full_capacity_year, else startup_year, else H, and no earlier than Y + 1; one dated after H is left out. Each scenario: growth = (its TWh in H ÷ A)^(1 ÷ (H − Y)) − 1, and utilisation = its TWh ÷ the TWh of the stock plus pipeline at full use. The scenarios are national, so regions are a breakdown, not compared one by one; the regional ESOO supply surplus is not used, as its maximum demand already includes AEMO's own data-centre forecast. as_of = the newest last_verified_at in projects.parquet. Also stale when half the risked-base MW was last checked over 120 days ago (the MW-weighted median last_verified_at). Change: the same needed growth, against the same scenarios, from the database as it stood 3 months before as_of (else 1 month), from its recorded history; series: at each month end.
Inputs
data/au_dc/processed/projects.parquet: nem_region, status, risked_mw, risk_base_mw, capacity_basis, pue, startup_year, full_capacity_year, last_verified_at
data/au_dc/reference/dc_demand_forecasts.csv: year, scenario, dc_consumption_twh
data/history/au_projects/index.csv: vintage_date, file
Refresh
AU lane: weekly rebuild of the project DB · owner AU lane · stale after 120 days
Notes
NEM regions: 38 operating projects record 1,641 MW (25 more disclose no MW and add nothing); the pipeline to 2032 is 2,598 MW risked of 8,879 MW unrisked; the pipeline outside the NEM (WA, 200 MW risked) shows only by region. full_capacity_year is recorded for 1 of 31 NEM pipeline projects, so 17 are placed by startup_year and 13 undated ones (609 MW risked) at 2032; as AEMO notes new facilities take five to ten years to reach a mature rate, the comparison is made at 2032, not year by year. PUE 1.32 is the median of the 49 NEM projects with MW that state one; at AEMO's 45–51% the needed growth is 26.9–29.2% a year, and on the 2025 actual the operating stock runs at 21% (AEMO: 27% of connection capacity in quarter 1 2026). Earlier values are the project database as it stood on each date, from its recorded history (data/history/au_projects, 13 versions since 13 Apr 2026), corrections included: a change can be a market move or a fix to the data. On 7 Jul 2026 it stood as recorded on 5 Jul 2026. The scenarios are today's file. 70 of 103 projects (96% of the MW) were checked in the last 120 days; half the MW was last checked on 7 Oct 2026.

How it is measured
Formula
Per operator: contracted_capacity_mw ÷ (operating_capacity_mw + under_construction_mw + future_build_mw), a blank part counted as 0, or ÷ total_capacity_mw where the row gives no breakdown; each operator's latest dated contracted figure is paired with its latest dated capacity figure. Market figure = Σ contracted ÷ Σ capacity over the operators with both, in %. Disclosing share = their capacity ÷ the capacity the file states across all its operators (each operator's latest figure). as_of = the newest announcement_date among the rows the market figure uses. The change is since the previous point, over the same operators. A row stated for a global, worldwide or international geography is left out of all of it: it is not an Australian figure.
