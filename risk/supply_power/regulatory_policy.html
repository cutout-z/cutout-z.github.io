Supply, demand & power

Regulation & policy

The rules that set the cost of power and the right to build and own: AEMC rule changes, AER network determinations, AEMO connection procedures, state planning, foreign-investment review and government hosting certification. The question for each change is whether it reaches assets already built and leased. A grid-augmentation charge, behind-the-meter obligation, data-centre levy or planning restriction applied retrospectively to existing assets raises the cost of running a facility under a lease already signed, while the same instrument applied to new entrants only is a moat for incumbents with secured power.

How it is measured
Formula
Count = items in regulatory_register.csv with affects_existing = yes, dated in the 12 months to as_of (as_of − 365 days < date ≤ as_of). affects_existing is what each item's source says: yes where it says the instrument applies to existing facilities or connections, no where it says new or expanded developments or new connections only, unclear where it says neither. as_of = the later of the register's newest item date and its last review (fetcher_log.json, regulatory_register.last_run; the newest item alone when that record is missing or unreadable). Change = the count at as_of less the count three months earlier (one month when the register does not reach back three). Series: the count at each quarter end and at as_of. Chart: one mark per item by date, in a lane per affects_existing reading (categorical colours, unclear in grey), with the 12-month window shaded.
Inputs
data/au_dc/reference/regulatory_register.csv: date, body, instrument, summary, affects_existing, source_url
data/fetcher_log.json: regulatory_register.last_run
Refresh
On event; Hermes's monthly curation pass · owner Hermes · stale after 90 days
Notes
10 items in the register, 10 of them dated in the 12 months to 2 Oct 2026, its last review: 2 that apply to existing facilities or connections as their sources state, 5 to new developments or connections only and 3 whose sources say neither. Items include proposals, consultations and announcements as well as made rules; the instrument column gives each one's stage. 2 such items are dated after 2 Jul 2026. The register was last reviewed on 2 Oct 2026, after its newest item (24 Sep 2026), so it is dated by that review.
