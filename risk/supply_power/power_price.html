Supply, demand & power

Power price

What powering a data centre in Australia costs: the delivered price by NEM region (wholesale energy plus network charges), how operators procure it (grid supply, power purchase agreements, on-site generation and storage), and how long new load waits to connect. Power is passed through to the tenant; a rising delivered price lowers the tenant's return on the site and is the lever a tenant pulls at renewal. The longer a new entrant waits to energise, the longer incumbents with connected power keep their pricing power.

How it is measured
Formula
Queue depth = the United States 'Queue Depth' row (GW of generation and storage seeking a grid connection, LBNL Queued Up) for the newest year in dc_queue_metrics.csv, read from the newest edition where a later one restates the year. Change = that minus the previous year's figure, since the previous year's published_date. Beside it: the United States 'Avg Wait Time' rows (years from connection request to commercial operation; the spec's 'median time-to-COD', the file's name kept). ERCOT and PJM Interconnect rows are listed in the table, not charted: single-market figures from other sources, not comparable with the national series. as_of = the published_date of the queue-depth row the value uses.
Inputs
data/reference/dc_queue_metrics.csv: region, year, metric, value, unit, source, published_date
Refresh
Annual: LBNL Queued Up edition (Hermes curates the file) · owner Hermes (curated file) · stale after 400 days
Notes
US queue depth for 2024 is 2,290 GW (LBNL Queued Up 2025 Edition, published 15 Dec 2025), 310 GW less than for 2023; the newest US Avg Wait Time is 4.0 years (2024). LBNL reports the wait from connection request to commercial operation as a median for a build cohort, and each edition's cohort differs, so a change between editions mixes cohort and method. LBNL counts generation and storage seeking a US grid connection, not data-centre load: a proxy for connection friction. The AU queue is S4-AU, beside it on this dossier.

How it is measured
Formula
Queue depth = the NSW1 queue_depth_mw row (MW applied for and not yet connected, AEMO Quarterly Energy Dynamics) at its newest date in connection_queue.csv; change = that minus the previous NSW1 figure, since its date. Beside it: each region's queue depth and median_days_to_connect (median days from application to connection, AEMO NEM Connections Scorecard), charted for NSW1, QLD1, VIC1, SA1 and TAS1; other rows, such as the NEM total, are tabled. GW, months and years are converted to MW and days. as_of = the newest NSW1 queue_depth_mw date.

How it is measured
Formula
Delivered price = wholesale (AEMO regional reference price, RRP) + the network charge in force (AER, where published), A$/MWh. Each region's rows are averaged by calendar day first; a network row applies from its date until the region's next one. Headline: NSW1, the mean daily delivered price over the 12 months to the newest price date; change = that minus the same 12-month average three months earlier. Beside it: quarterly averages by region, wholesale and network stacked, and each region's latest full quarter against the same quarter a year earlier (YoY). as_of = the newest NSW1 wholesale date.
Inputs
data/au_dc/processed/nem_prices.parquet: date, nem_region, component, price_aud_mwh
Refresh
AU lane: weekly NEMOSIS pull of AEMO's monthly archives · owner AU lane (Hermes, H6) · stale after 45 days
Notes
NSW1, 365 days of prices in the 12 months to 31 Aug 2026: wholesale A$75.0/MWh; no network row is in force for NSW1, so this is wholesale alone. A rolling year, so the winter peak does not read as a change; each region's latest full quarter against the same quarter a year earlier is in the table. NSW1's last bar, 2026 Q3, is a partial quarter, 62 of its 92 days; a partial quarter's bar is drawn lighter.
