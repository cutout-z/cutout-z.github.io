Supply, demand & power

Demand & grid

Demand for data-centre power in Australia and the grid that serves it: AEMO's Electricity Statement of Opportunities, NEM demand by region, and the published data-centre consumption scenarios the pipeline is measured against. Demand that grows slower than the pipeline leaves a tenant more alternatives when its lease comes up for renewal; demand that outgrows it does the reverse. Grid headroom works on the supply side: where the grid has little spare capacity, less competing capacity can connect and run.
